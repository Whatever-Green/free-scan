Freescan

The final chapter.

Thank you for using Freescan.

After the final exams finish, Freescan will be shutting down. Since 2022, it has been an honour helping students find textbooks, resources, and revision material when they needed it most.

What started as a simple idea became something that supported people through GCSEs, A-Levels, late-night revision sessions, and the chaos of exam season.

Freescan may be ending in this form, but its purpose is not disappearing. Sometime in the future, it will return in a different shape within education — with the same aim of making learning easier to access.
